Music: Cambridge Audio EVO ONE

The Cambridge Audio EVO ONE is the all-in-one speaker system in the living area. It sounds fantastic, so enjoy it.

Easiest way: Bluetooth

  1. Press the Bluetooth button (or turn the source dial to Bluetooth) so it's in pairing mode.
  2. On your phone, open Settings → Bluetooth and select EVO ONE.
  3. Play from any app: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, whatever you like.

Better quality: AirPlay, Chromecast or Spotify Connect

  • Make sure your phone is on the house Wi-Fi.
  • In Spotify, tap the speaker/Connect icon and choose the EVO ONE.
  • On iPhone, use AirPlay from Control Centre and pick the EVO ONE.

The Victoria Point Sharks

Aussie Rules is a big part of local life here, and the home team is flying.

The Redland–Victoria Point Sharks ("the Sharks") are the local Aussie Rules club, founded in 1966 and playing in the QAFL, and they were crowned 2025 QAFL Premiers.

  • Home ground: Scottsdale Park, Victoria Point.
  • Home games: Saturdays through the season (roughly April–September). Catching a game, with a drink and a burger from the clubhouse, is a great local afternoon.
  • Training: Tuesday & Thursday nights from 6pm if you fancy watching a run.
  • The Sharks Sporting Club is also a social hub with bar, meals and events, open to visitors.

Getting Around by Bus

Public transport across the Redlands is run by TransLink. Victoria Point has a bus interchange near the shopping centre, with regular services to Cleveland (for the train to Brisbane) and around the local area.

  • Local routes run roughly every 30 minutes on weekdays (less often evenings & weekends).
  • Pay by tapping on and off with a go card or a Visa/Mastercard, the same as the ferry.
  • Heading into the city? Take a bus to Cleveland station and connect to the Brisbane train line.

Please note. Timetables change, so always check live departures before you head out.

A Bit of History

The rich red soil of the Redlands drew farming families here in the late 1800s. Early settlers cleared the scrub to grow pineapples, bananas and vegetables, and much of the district began as farmland and bay-side homesteads (the historic Nura estate sat out near Point Halloran). The first little school opened in 1877, with the State school following in 1916.

Today it's a relaxed bayside community, and if you hear a local mention "Vicky Point," that's just the affectionate shorthand everyone round here uses for Victoria Point. Now you're in on it too.
